## Unresolved source discrepancies
The brochure refers to both **more than 1,024 beds** (page 9) and **1,043 teaching beds** (page 10). V25 **does not place either figure in headline stats**. Resolve with the institution's signed current capacity data before publication.

The brochure includes dated attendance, bond, disciplinary and other policies, as well as student portal details mentioning a default PIN. These are not copied into the public website as current instructions. Legal / regulatory publication requires fresh institutional review. **Do not publish the default PIN.**

## Dental and nursing data not available in this source
The supplied brochure is for the Medical College/Hospital. It does not substantiate dental college or dental hospital service lists, BDS/MDS programmes, dental faculty rosters, dental facilities, or nursing programme approvals. The five-site product structure is user-provided; the missing specifics remain pending institutional verification.

## Photography usage
Official SVS group-supplied media assets were used as supplied, with descriptive and conservative captions. Medical-college, hospital and nursing media are distinguishable. Dental pages use **clearly labeled SVS group-context photography**, not a claim that the picture is a dental clinic or dental college. Actual dental imagery is a production content dependency.

## Editorial rule
Human-readable descriptive writing is permitted when it restates the institution's documented history, activities or the site's navigation purpose. Marketing superlatives, unverified outcomes, fake testimonials, programme numbers, doctor availability and patient-care promises were not inserted.
